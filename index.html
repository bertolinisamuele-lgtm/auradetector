PRIVACY & DATA

In plain language

What we collect. For a photo scan, the image you choose is processed to produce your result. For a scenario scan, the text you type. Locally we also store your results on this device so you can see your history.

Why. Only to generate and show your Aura Score, and (if enabled) to keep your personal history.

How long. Your uploaded image is used for analysis and is not kept by the app after your result is produced. History stores only the result data (score, categories, archetype, summary) — not the original photo.

Who we share with. If a real AI provider is connected, the content you submit is sent to that provider solely to produce the result. We do not sell your data.

Delete my data. Clears your local history and any stored result data on this device.

Delete my account. Removes your local account profile and all associated local data.

Request deletion / GDPR. EU users can request access, correction or deletion of their data by contacting privacy@auradetector.app. We aim to respond within 30 days.

We implement security best practices and data minimisation. We do not claim to be “100% hacker-proof”.
